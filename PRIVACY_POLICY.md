# Privacy Policy for Roatan Domino

**Effective Date:** November 20, 2026  
**Developer:** RoatanDomino (`com.roatandomino.game`)  
**Contact:** amsonmurillo10@gmail.com

## 1. Overview
Roatan Domino is a dominoes board game offering Offline play against AI bots and optional Online multiplayer. We respect your privacy and collect only the minimal information necessary to operate the game.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Offline Mode:** Offline gameplay requires **no account**, **no internet connection**, and sends **no personal data** off your device. Game preferences (selected language, sound toggle, mode, bot difficulty, and local offline win/match counters) are stored locally on your device via Unity `PlayerPrefs`.
- **Optional Online Mode:** If you choose to connect to a multiplayer server and create an account, you provide a username, email address, password (stored as a cryptographic hash), and birthdate so you can sign in, join lobbies, and play matches.
- **No Sensitive Tracking:** Roatan Domino does not collect precise GPS location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or third-party advertising identifiers.

## 3. How We Use Information
- To save your local game settings and offline statistics on your device.
- To authenticate your account and synchronize multiplayer lobbies, chat, and match state when you connect to an online server.

## 4. Data Sharing & Third Parties
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Data is never shared except as required to operate a multiplayer server you explicitly choose to connect to, or to comply with applicable law.

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion
- Local offline preferences and statistics can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Roatan Domino.
- To request deletion of an online account or associated data, email **amsonmurillo10@gmail.com** with your username and registered email address.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Roatan Domino is a classic family-friendly board game suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe a child has submitted personal data, please contact us to have it promptly removed.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy as new features are added. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
